# Human-led, AI-assisted software development

**Project:** WeldVision AI (research-oriented macrograph measurements and segmentation).

On 17 November 2026, maintainer **@gregoryou** reported that the research-software idea, functional requirements, project direction, hands-on acceptance tests, and physical weld-image verification were conducted by the maintainer. ChatGPT and other AI tools assisted with software implementation, changes and documentation. The maintainer reported no knowingly copied code from third parties or a university-owned project.

This is the maintainer's statement, **not** independent verification of chain-of-title or intellectual-property rights. AI tools are acknowledged as implementation aids rather than human authors. Third-party code, dependencies and model checkpoints retain their own rights and licenses.

**Version 3.5.0 (20 November 2026):** the source archive includes an extended PL/EN GUI, SAM Huge selection, mask correction, geometric measurement editor and additional tests. This note does not imply an independent rights audit of every subsequent contribution.

The original source code, insofar as the maintainer has rights to license it, is provided under **GNU GPL-3.0-only**. Research images and pretrained model weights are not distributed in this archive.
